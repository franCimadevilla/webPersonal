Help and site map

This page explains how the site is organised and how to get around it with a mouse, a finger or just the keyboard.

Site map

The site has five pages, in this order:

  1. Home: introduction and contact.
    • Introduction
    • Get in touch (email, GitHub, LinkedIn and form)
  2. About me: my story, what I am doing now and my hobbies.
    • My story
    • Right now
    • Interests and hobbies
  3. Projects: education, experience, technologies, projects, references and CV.
    • Education
    • Experience
    • Tech stack

  4. Multimedia: demo videos of my projects and a podcast.
    • Videos
    • Podcast of interest
  5. Gallery: photos of places I have visited.

This help page is not part of that sequence; you can open it from the footer of any page.

How to get around

  • Top menu: takes you to any of the five pages. The page you are on is highlighted.
  • Breadcrumbs: above the title of each inner page («Home › Projects») they show where you are and take you back to the home page.
  • Bottom carousel: the «Previous» and «Next» links say which page they lead to, and each dot is a page, numbered from 1 (Home) to 5 (Gallery). The highlighted dot is the current page.
  • Side arrows: on wide screens, the ‹ and › strips at the sides lead to the previous and the next page. From 1200 px wide they show a blurred slice of those pages, with their name, and clicking them slides the carousel to that side. On mobile you can also change pages by swiping sideways.
  • Looping sequence: after Gallery comes Home again, and before Home comes Gallery. It works the same in the carousel, with the side arrows and with the keyboard.
  • Search: the «Search» link in the header (on mobile, the magnifying glass) opens the search page, which finds any word on the pages of the site.
  • Language: the Español link in the header opens the same page in Spanish, and English brings you back to the English version.
  • Animations: the phrase on the home page types itself over and over; the «Pause animation» checkbox stops it. If your system is set to reduce motion (on Windows, with animation effects turned off), pages change without sliding.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ← and →: previous and next page, on the five pages of the sequence. The shortcut does nothing if you also press Alt, Ctrl, Shift or Cmd, or while you type in a field or use a video or audio player: there the arrows work as usual.
  • Tab and Shift + Tab: next and previous element. The active element is marked with a blue outline.
  • The first time you press Tab, a «Skip to content» link appears; it skips the menu and goes straight to the page text.
  • Enter: opens the selected link or presses the selected button.
